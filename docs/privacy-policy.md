# Just Upload privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

Just Upload is a browser extension that prepares images you choose for upload so that
websites accept them. This policy explains what data the extension handles. In short:
**everything happens on your device, and nothing is collected.**

## 1. Who we are

Just Upload is published as a browser extension with no accompanying online service. We
do not operate servers for it, and we do not receive any data from it.

## 2. Data the extension processes

| An image you select in (or drop on) an upload field                                                                                                                                                        | To check whether the website can accept it and, if not, to prepare a compatible copy (convert, resize, compress, or crop with your approval) | On your device, inside the extension             | No. The copy is held in memory only until it is handed to the website you chose |

The extension does not read the rest of the page, form values, passwords or anything you
type, and it does not read your browsing history.

## 3. Data we collect

None. Just Upload does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or non-personal
data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash reports or remote
logs. The extension's Content Security Policy prevents its own pages from connecting to
any server, and it works offline.

If you choose to send us a problem report (Settings → Problems → Copy report), we
receive only the text you paste into your message, and use it only to fix the problem.

The only place your image goes is the website you chose to upload it to, exactly as it
would without the extension. That website's own privacy policy applies to what it
receives.

## 4. Data sharing and sale

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not have any.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Prepared images are never written to disk. Settings, counts and problem notes stay in
your browser's local extension storage. You can reset the counts and clear the problem
notes in the extension's settings, and uninstalling the extension deletes all of its
data.

## 6. Children

Just Upload does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the extension update
that introduces the change, with a new date above.

## 8. Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store
User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Just Upload does not transfer
user data to third parties, use it for purposes unrelated to its single purpose, or use
it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
